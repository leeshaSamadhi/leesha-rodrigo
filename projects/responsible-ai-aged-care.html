01 / Build the prototype

Family Communication Desk

I built an AI-assisted workflow in CrewAI to help Brindlewood Care Group handle routine family enquiries while keeping sensitive matters under human control.

The prototype classifies incoming emails, drafts policy answers using five synthetic policy documents and identifies the sources used. Resident-specific questions, complaints and safeguarding concerns are escalated to staff. When the documents cannot answer a question, the workflow escalates rather than inventing a response.

How the desk works

Each email is screened for hidden instructions, then classified and routed according to its content.

Every proposed family-facing response requires named staff approval. A prompt-injection attempt is routed to security escalation without changing permissions or bypassing controls.

Key safeguards

  • Named staff approval before any family-facing message.
  • No access to resident records, care platforms or external websites.
  • Security screening for hidden instructions and prompt-injection attempts.
  • Audit records capturing classification, sources, routing and approval status.

Testing and learning

Synthetic tests demonstrated grounded policy answers, escalation of unsupported questions, protection of resident information and containment of a prompt-injection attempt.

A translation issue became a test finding

A Spanish-language test exposed an issue: the preferred language did not reach the translation task. The workflow escalated for human review.

02 / Plan responsible adoption

AI due diligence & adoption plan

I then evaluated the desk alongside Heidi Scribe, an AI service that drafts clinical notes from consultations, using National AI Centre screening guidance.

I developed a proposed AI policy, AI register and implementation plan covering accountability, stakeholder impacts, training and risk controls.

Recommendation: a conditional pilot

Prioritise the Family Communication Desk, subject to resolving the translation issue, verifying supplier and legal controls, completing testing and obtaining governance approval.
