UI/UX design / MVP development

Medi App.
Healthcare, designed around the patient.

I developed the business case, high-fidelity Figma design and MVP architecture for a medical app, then brought the experience to life with Cline using structured S.C.A.F.F. prompts.

01 / The problem

One patient. Too many disconnected tools.

The project addresses a fragmented healthcare experience: separate tools for appointments, records and communication, with complex interfaces that can add stress to medical interactions.

Medi App brings these activities into one patient-centred mobile experience, designed to feel calm and easier to navigate. Reducing anxiety was a design goal, rather than a measured outcome.

02 / My contribution

From business case to interactive MVP

  • Defined the business case around fragmented patient journeys and the need for a more integrated experience.
  • Created the high-fidelity Figma prototype and MVP architecture, connecting the core healthcare tasks in a mobile design.
  • Used AI-assisted development with Cline to implement the experience in React, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS, with React Router v6.
  • Structured prompts around acceptance criteria using the S.C.A.F.F. method to specify the context, required action, output and validation.

03 / MVP scope

Six connected feature areas

Login

Basic email and password login.

Appointment scheduling

Book, reschedule and cancel appointments within the app.

Doctor discovery & ratings

Browse by specialty, gender, rating and favourites.

GP chat

Direct messaging with voice-note support, intended to reduce the need for clinic visits.

Medical records & notes

A central place for patients to access their health history.

In-app payment

A payment experience covering card, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal options.

Project stage: this is a design and MVP project. These features describe its scope, not verified production integrations or clinical deployment.

04 / Prompting methodology

Giving Cline a clear brief

I used S.C.A.F.F. to turn design intent and user acceptance criteria into structured development prompts.

S

Situation

Set the context: the app, existing work and the React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and React Router v6 stack.

C

Complication

Describe the specific problem or gap the prompt needs to solve.

A

Action

Give Cline clear instructions, including the Medi App user acceptance criteria.

F

Format

Specify the expected output structure, file names or code style.

F

Feedback

Define how to validate the output and confirm it meets the request.

05 / Business perspective

Assessing the concept with SWOT

My SWOT analysis identified the calming interface and integrated features as strengths. It also highlighted the MVP’s limited scalability and dependence on doctor onboarding. Potential opportunities included Melbourne healthcare centres and hospital partnerships, alongside threats from established competitors, privacy and compliance concerns, and patient trust.

06 / Key learnings

Key learnings

  • Business case definition: framing a product around a clear patient problem and an explicit MVP scope.
  • UI/UX design: translating connected healthcare tasks into a high-fidelity mobile experience with a calm visual direction.
  • Structured prompting: using S.C.A.F.F. to explain context, acceptance criteria and expected outputs to an AI coding assistant.
  • Testing: defining how generated outputs should be checked through the Feedback step.
